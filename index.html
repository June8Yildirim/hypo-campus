Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 29-Apr-2026

App Version: 2.0.1

This Privacy Policy describes how Hypo-Campus ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to keeping your data private — your flashcards and study history stay on your device and iCloud account.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data You Provide

  • Flashcard Content: Front and back text, card type (Classic, Multiple Choice, True/False, Fill-in-the-Blank), and card images you capture with your camera
  • Deck Information: Deck names and creation dates you define
  • Review Ratings: How you grade each card (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) and when each review occurs
  • App Preferences: Theme selection, view styles, shuffle settings, and other customization options

1.2 Automatically Generated Data

  • Streak & Review History: Daily review counts and consecutive-day streaks derived from your review activity
  • Achievement Progress: Milestones unlocked based on review volume, streaks, and cram sessions
  • Scheduled Reminders: Notification delivery times calculated from your review schedule

1.3 AI-Generated Content (Optional Feature)

When you use the Create with AI feature, the topic or text you enter is sent to Google's Gemini API (via Firebase Vertex AI) to generate flashcard suggestions. This data is transmitted over HTTPS and processed by Google according to their privacy policy. No other personal data is sent to Google.

1.4 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Health Data: We do not access HealthKit or any health-related information
  • Location Data: We do not collect or request your location at any time
  • Contacts: We do not access your address book or contact list
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, or Apple ID
  • Microphone / Audio: We do not record audio
  • Analytics / Crash Reports: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs that profile your behaviour

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Primary Uses

  • To display your flashcard decks and power spaced-repetition scheduling
  • To track your daily review streak, achievements, and study statistics
  • To deliver local push notifications reminding you to review due cards
  • To synchronize your data across your own devices via iCloud (if enabled)
  • To generate AI flashcard suggestions when you explicitly request them

2.2 Data Usage Principles

  • Data is used solely to provide app functionality
  • We do not share, sell, or monetise your data in any way
  • We do not use your data for advertising or user profiling
  • All data remains under your control and can be deleted at any time

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All flashcard content, deck data, and card images are stored locally on your device using CoreData. Your data remains on your device unless you enable iCloud sync.

3.2 iCloud / CloudKit Sync (Optional)

  • If enabled, your flashcard decks and card data are synchronised to your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit
  • Review history, achievements, and streak-freeze charges are synchronised via iCloud Key-Value Store
  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Apple's iCloud security infrastructure
  • We do not have access to your iCloud data — it is stored in your personal iCloud container

3.3 Camera Photos

Photos you take with your camera for card images are stored as compressed JPEG data directly inside your local CoreData store (and optionally synced via iCloud). Photos are never uploaded to our servers or any third party other than your own iCloud account.

3.4 Data Security

  • All data is protected by iOS and iCloud encryption features
  • We do not operate our own servers — no data is transmitted to us
  • Push notification tokens are handled entirely by Apple's APNs infrastructure

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Gemini / Firebase Vertex AI

The optional Create with AI feature uses Google's Gemini language model via Firebase Vertex AI. When you use this feature, the text you type as a card topic is sent to Google's servers to generate suggestions. This is the only data sent outside your device. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Terms of Service.

You can use Hypo-Campus fully without ever using the AI feature — all other functionality is entirely on-device.

4.2 Apple Services

  • iCloud / CloudKit: Optional cross-device sync governed by Apple's iCloud Terms and Conditions
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs): Used to deliver local review reminders; no personal content is included in notification payloads

4.3 No Other Third Parties

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs (e.g., Amplitude, Mixpanel, Firebase Analytics), social media SDKs, or any other third-party data collectors.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

  • You can view all your flashcard and deck data directly within the app
  • You can edit or delete any card, deck, or review entry at any time
  • You can reset achievement progress by deleting and reinstalling the app

5.2 iCloud Sync

You can disable iCloud sync in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Hypo-Campus. Disabling sync stops synchronisation but does not delete local data on your device.

5.3 Camera Permission

Camera access is requested only when you tap the camera button inside the card editor. You can revoke camera permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Hypo-Campus. The app remains fully functional without camera access.

5.4 Push Notifications

You can disable review reminder notifications in iOS Settings → Notifications → Hypo-Campus or within the app's Settings screen.

5.5 Data Deletion

  • Deleting the app from your device removes all local CoreData and UserDefaults data immediately
  • iCloud data can be deleted via iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Hypo-Campus
  • We do not retain any data after app deletion because we never receive it in the first place

6. Children's Privacy

Hypo-Campus is rated 4+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because all data is stored on-device or in the user's own iCloud account and we do not receive any user data on our servers, this risk is minimal. If you have concerns, please contact us at the address below.

7. Push Notifications and Background Processing

Hypo-Campus uses push notifications and background processing to:

  • Remind you when cards are due for review
  • Alert you when your review streak is at risk
  • Synchronise data with iCloud in the background

Background activity occurs only when necessary. No additional personal information is collected during background processing. Notification content is generated locally on your device and does not pass through our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Posting a notice within the app for significant changes

Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained on your device for as long as you use the app
  • Review history stored in iCloud KV Store is automatically pruned to the last 365 days
  • Deleting the app removes all local data immediately
  • We do not retain any data on our own servers because we never receive it

10. International Users

If you use Hypo-Campus from outside the United States, please note that:

  • iCloud data is stored in Apple's infrastructure, which may be located in different regions depending on your Apple ID country
  • AI feature requests (Gemini) are processed by Google, which may transfer data internationally
  • By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your data as described in this policy

Hypo-Campus — Development Progress

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Overview

Hypo-Campus grows from a basic flashcard MVP (1.0) to today's 2.3.1 in 13 releases, plus two Android releases. Each release ships one feature theme, and patch releases (x.x.1) hold the bug fixes. Versions are grouped by feature, not by commit date, so they don't match the git history exactly.

1.0 — MVP: core flashcards

The smallest app that works end to end: make a deck, add cards, review them.

Features

  • Create, rename and delete decks
  • Note cards (question and answer), entered by hand
  • Tap a card to flip it; swipe between cards
  • Four review buttons with fixed intervals: Critical (1 day), Soon (3 days), Stable (5 days), Mastered (7 days)
  • Local storage on the device

1.1 — Markdown import and export

Features

  • Import a .md file and turn it into a deck
  • Markdown parser for card blocks
  • Export a deck back to a Markdown file and share it

1.2 — Review queue and reminders

Features

  • Review queue that groups due cards by bucket (Critical, Soon, Stable, Mastered)
  • Review sessions that work through one bucket at a time
  • Daily reminder notifications when cards are due

1.3 — Richer cards

Features

  • Multiple-choice cards
  • Fill-in-the-blank cards
  • Markdown formatting and LaTeX maths on both sides of a card
  • Card type picker when adding a card

1.4 — AI card creation

Features

  • Generate a set of cards from a topic or pasted notes with Google Gemini
  • Review and edit generated cards before saving them to a deck

1.5 — Library organisation

Features

  • Subjects that group related decks
  • Three deck views: list, wheel and grid
  • Sort decks by recently reviewed, alphabetical or card count
  • Deck detail screen with its card list

1.6 — Statistics

Features

  • 12-week activity heatmap
  • 7-day review chart and upcoming-due chart (tomorrow, next four days)
  • Per-deck stats and a review log

1.6.1 — Stability fixes

Bug fixes

  • Review queue showing stale or duplicate cards
  • LaTeX not rendering in some cards
  • Notifications not firing reliably
  • Wheel and list deck views not showing on macOS

2.0 — Smart scheduling

The major release: reviews adapt to how well each card is known, instead of using fixed intervals.

Features

  • SM-2 adaptive intervals with a per-card ease factor
  • FSRS-4.5 as a second algorithm, selectable in Settings
  • Cram mode to drill a deck without changing its schedule
  • Redesigned review buttons, with undo for the last rating

2.1 — Personalisation

Features

  • 8 colour themes
  • Card animation styles: 3D flip, slide or none
  • Card font weight (9 options) and width (4 options)
  • Settings grouped into categories: Appearance, Cards, Editing, Decks, Review
  • Forced-update prompt when a required new version is out

2.2 — Motivation

Features

  • 24 achievements: review milestones (1 to 10,000), daily goals, perfect day, cram sessions
  • Daily streaks from 3 to 365 days, with a streak freeze
  • Home-screen widget that shows a card of the day

2.3 — Photos and new card types

Features

  • Add photos to cards from the camera or photo library, with a full-screen viewer
  • True/false cards
  • Fill-in-the-blank cards with a dropdown answer menu
  • Markdown import for the new card types

2.3.1 — Performance and polish

Bug fixes

  • 1–3 second freeze after tapping a review button (queue refreshes are now batched)
  • Keyboard covering text fields when adding cards
  • Jerky deck wheel animation
  • Card view layout tidied up

Android 2.3.1 — New platform

The Android app starts at 2.3.1 so both platforms share one version number. It matches the iOS feature set except AI card creation.

Features

  • Kotlin and Jetpack Compose app for Android 8.0 and later
  • Every iOS feature from 1.0 to 2.3 except AI card creation: all five card types, SM-2 and FSRS, cram mode, Markdown and LaTeX, import and export, photos, stats, achievements, 8 themes, notifications and the widget
  • Data stays on the device; no ads and no advertising ID

Bug fixes found while porting

  • Tapping card text didn't flip the card
  • Heatmap day labels were clipped
  • Only the first line of a multi-line text field could be tapped

Android 2.3.1 (build 4) — Play Store readiness

Fixes found while preparing the Play Console upload and testing on the emulator. The version name stays 2.3.1; the version code is 4.

Bug fixes

  • Midnight theme: primary colour was almost invisible on the dark background (now #5288C0)
  • Exported Markdown couldn't be imported back; export now writes the import format for all five card types
  • Exporting an empty deck now shows "Nothing to Export" instead of sharing an empty file
  • Review reminders were lost after a force-stop until the next card was rated; they're now rescheduled on every launch
  • Notification icon showed as a solid blob; it's now a monochrome card icon
  • Widget showed raw Markdown symbols such as backticks

Play Console

  • The manifest removes the AD_ID permission so no library can add it back, matching a "No" answer on the Advertising ID form
  • Version code raised to 4 for the next upload

Testing

  • Round-trip unit test: export a deck with every card type, import it, and check each card matches
  • Checked on an Android 15 emulator: a reminder fires and opens its review queue, the widget works on the home screen, the camera adds a photo to a card, and export opens the share sheet
